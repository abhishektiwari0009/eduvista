Erasmus Mundus Joint Master's Scholarship
Fully Funded
  • Financial Cover: Full Tuition Fees
  • Living Stipend: Rs. 1,400 / month
  • Inclusions: Global travel allowance & health insurance coverage

A high-level integrated study program delivered by a consortium of EU universities. Students study in at least two different European countries, leading to joint or double degrees.

DAAD Graduate Scholarship (Germany)
Fully Funded
  • Financial Cover: Full Tuition Fees at state-funded universities
  • Living Allowance: Rs. 934+ / month (Master's: Rs. 934, PhD: Rs. 1,200)
  • Inclusions: Travel allowance, health/accident insurance, study subsidy

Offered by the German Academic Exchange Service, this scholarship supports postgraduate students from developing countries to pursue developmental-focused degrees at leading German universities.

Eiffel Excellence Scholarship (France)
Fully Funded
  • Financial Cover: Institutional enrollment fees
  • Monthly Stipend: Rs. 1,181 / month (Master's) | Rs. 1,700 / month (PhD)
  • Inclusions: International travel, local transport, health coverage

Established by the French Ministry of Europe and Foreign Affairs, this program aims to attract outstanding international candidates for Master's and PhD programs in French higher education institutions.

🇸🇪
Swedish Institute (SI) Scholarship
Fully Funded
  • Financial Cover: Full Tuition Fees
  • Living Stipend: SEK 11,000 / month
  • Inclusions: Travel grant (SEK 15,000), health insurance, SI network membership

The Global Professionals program, funded by the Swedish Ministry for Foreign Affairs, targets highly-qualified students from selected countries pursuing full-time Master's degrees in Sweden.

Swiss Government Excellence Scholarship
Fully Funded

  • Living Stipend: CHF 1,920 / month
  • Inclusions: Housing assistance, mandatory health insurance, flight allowance

Aimed at international post-graduate researchers and artists, this highly prestigious scholarship promotes research cooperation and exchange between Switzerland and over 180 countries.

Italian Government Scholarship
Fully Funded
  • Financial Cover: Tuition fee exemption
  • Living Stipend: Rs. 900 / month
  • Inclusions: Comprehensive health and medical insurance

Offered by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation (MAECI) to encourage international cooperation and promote Italian culture and language.

Austrian Government Scholarship
Fully Funded
  • Financial Cover: Tuition fees waived
  • Living Stipend: Rs. 1,050 / month
  • Inclusions: Health insurance, travel cost compensation

Granted by the Austrian Federal Ministry of Education, Science and Research to support study or research projects in Austria at universities of applied sciences or research institutions.

🇳🇱
Holland Scholarship (Netherlands)
Partial Grant
  • Financial Cover: One-time grant of Rs. 5,000
  • Target Group: Non-EEA international students
  • Level: First-year Bachelor's or Master's studies

Co-financed by the Dutch Ministry of Education and over 30 Dutch research universities, this grant provides financial support to excellent non-European students during their first year.

Danish Government Scholarship
Full / Partial Waiver
  • Financial Cover: Full or partial tuition fee waiver
  • Living Allowance: Contribution to basic living costs

Awarded directly by Danish universities on the basis of academic excellence. Students do not apply separately; all eligible university applicants are automatically pre-screened.

Program FAQs

Q1. How will Standyou Scholarship Assistance Program help me in getting a Scholarship?
Standyou Scholarship Assistance Program will give you an edge over your competitors. This program prepares your profile to get eligible for the scholarships. This program suggests the best scholarship for you based on your profile and also guides you with the complete process of application along with the timeline. Your personalized scholarship report covers suggested scholarships, eligibility in terms of academic and non-academic, timeline, the complete process of application, and documentation. It will also cover the SOP, LOR, work experience, CV, essay, publications, suggested course programs, interview preparations and other requirements to avail the best scholarship for your profile.
Q2. What are the study destinations covered under Standyou Scholarship Assistance Program?
Our program covers seven major global educational destinations, including Canada, Germany, Italy, South Korea, Sweden, the United Kingdom (UK), and the United States of America (USA).
Q3. What are the Parameters for getting a Scholarship?
Scholarship decisions are based on multiple factors. Key parameters include your academic GPA, English language test scores (IELTS/TOEFL/PTE), standardized tests (GRE/GMAT/SAT), quality of your Statement of Purpose (SOP) and Letters of Recommendation (LOR), extracurricular achievements, research publications, and financial background or need.
Q4. Will this Program guarantee a Scholarship Award?
No program can guarantee a scholarship award since disbursements are decided by university boards and government panels. However, the program increases your chances to more than 95% by aligning your profile, identifying matched opportunities, ensuring zero documentation errors, and guiding your essays.
Q5. What will be the amount of the Scholarship? Will it cover complete study expenses?
Scholarships range from partial tuition waivers (10% to 50%) to full-tuition coverage. Some prestigious bursaries, such as Commonwealth or Erasmus+, are fully funded—covering complete tuition fees, monthly living stipends, and accommodation costs.
Q6. What is Profiling Session?
A profiling session is a 1:1 live video session with our data mining experts. We record, discuss, and analyze your academic background, test scores, research work, and career aspirations across 150+ metrics.
Q7. What is Report Analysis Session?
During this 1:1 live session, a scholarship consultant walks you through your Personalized Scholarship Report. They outline the matched scholarship options, draft strategies for applications, and explain how to refine your SOP, CV, and academic profile.
Q8. What will be the mode of the Profiling & Scholarship Report Analysis Session?
All profiling and report analysis sessions are conducted completely online via 1:1 interactive live video calls for your comfort and convenience.
Q9. What will be in the Report and how will it help me in getting the scholarship?
The report maps out a list of 20 best-fit scholarship opportunities (including primary, secondary, university, and accommodation options), step-by-step application timelines, and specific recommendations on academic improvements, SOP drafts, LOR strategies, and publication opportunities.
Q10. Will the Standyou Scholarship Assistance Program Cover Career Counselling?
Yes. The report includes career growth analyses, financial return on investment (ROI), post-study visa options, residency pathways, part-time work rules, and destination-wise job placement metrics.
Q11. How many study destinations and Scholarships will get covered under Standyou Scholarship Assistance Program?
We cover seven major countries (UK, USA, Canada, Germany, Italy, Sweden, South Korea) and run your student profile against our massive global database of over 50,000 active scholarships and grants.
Q12. My GPA/Academic score is low, Can I still get the scholarship?
Yes! Many international scholarships evaluate students holistically rather than just looking at grades. High test scores, compelling essays, active community service, or research papers can compensate for lower GPAs. Our profiling session will find scholarships that align with your specific strengths.
Q13. What in case I face any issue or difficulty at the time of filling out the application or documentation?
Our consultants remain in contact with you throughout the application cycle. We offer dedicated support for documentation reviews, application filing assistance, and timeline reminders so you never face issues or miss deadlines.
